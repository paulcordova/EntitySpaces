# Archived licensing documents

These files are **historical**. They are kept for reference only and **do not apply** to the current
distribution of EntitySpaces.

| File | What it was | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `OFFICIAL LICENSE.txt` | Commercial EULA from EntitySpaces, LLC (2005-2012): per-developer-seat licenses, "no refunds", royalty-free redistributables | **Revoked.** Superseded by BSD 3-Clause |
| `BETA LICENSE AGREEMENT.rtf` | Beta program agreement from the same commercial era | **Revoked.** Superseded by BSD 3-Clause |

## Why they are here and not deleted

They document what the license terms were before the project was released as open source, which is
useful if a customer asks about the provenance of a specific historical version, or if a legal
review needs to reconstruct the chain of title.

Deleting them would not change anything legally — the terms stopped applying when the code was
published under the New BSD License — but keeping them with this notice preserves the record without
ambiguity.

## What applies today

The current license is **BSD 3-Clause**, stated in the [`LICENSE`](../../LICENSE) file at the root of
this repository. Scope, provenance and trademark notes are in [`NOTICE`](../../NOTICE). Third-party
component licenses are in [`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`](../../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

EntitySpaces Studio 2026.11 presents the BSD 3-Clause license in its installer. The NuGet packages
declare `BSD-3-Clause` through `<PackageLicenseExpression>`.

## If you are looking for commercial terms

There are none embedded in the software. Commercial support, consulting and training are available
separately from the maintainer and are governed by their own agreements.
